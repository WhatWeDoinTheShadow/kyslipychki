A caricature you laugh at together

A regular caricature enlarges the nose and ears. Ours enlarges the circumstances. If someone's week feels like an autumn storm, the painting gives them their own little rain cloud. If they're drowning in deadlines, they sit on a mountain of paperwork with a cup of coffee. The person stays themselves, and lovely; the situation becomes funny.

That's why you can give this caricature even to someone who isn't feeling great. It doesn't say "cheer up", it says "I see how it is for you, and I'm here". You'll find before-and-after examples on the Sour Mugs home page.

How we paint a caricature from a photo

  1. You send a photo and a few words: what's going on, what they love, what to avoid.
  2. The artist comes up with a scene from those details and paints it on canvas in oil, acrylic or pastel.
  3. The painting is shipped on its stretcher, ready to hang. Usually 3–5 days to paint, 48 hours with rush, plus delivery.

Questions about caricatures from photos

How is your caricature different?

A classic caricature exaggerates facial features. We exaggerate the situation: a personal cloud overhead, a mountain of to-dos, a blanket fortress. The person stays recognisable and lovely; the world around them is funny.

Is the caricature drawn on a tablet?

No. Every caricature is painted by hand with real paint on a stretched canvas. No tablet, printing or AI.

How much does a caricature from a photo cost?

From $55 / £42 / €49 for a 20×20 cm canvas, $85 / £65 / €75 for 30×40 cm and $129 / £99 / €115 for 50×70 cm. Rush in 48 hours: +$22 / £16 / €19. Shipping is quoted for your country.

What if they get offended?

We paint with love and never joke about looks. In the form you can tell us which topics to avoid. Whether and when to give it is up to you: the painting is delivered to you.

Can I order one of myself?

Of course. Sometimes the best thing to do with a bad day is to hang it on the wall and laugh at it.
