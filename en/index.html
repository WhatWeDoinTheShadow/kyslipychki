How sadness becomes a painting

We were sent a selfie from under a blanket: glasses, a pout, a "leave me alone" look. The artist didn't prettify it or mock it. They simply exaggerated what was already in the photo.

  • The glasses took centre stageA thin frame became bright yellow and half the size of the face.
  • The pout stayedOne red smudge and the mood needs no words.
  • The choker started drippingA tiny detail from the photo became the funniest thing on the canvas.

Who it's for

A friend going through a hard time

You can tell they're not themselves. Talking is hard, but you want to do something. Send us their sad photo and a few words about them, and we'll paint their portrait on canvas.

Yourself, when you need a laugh

Sometimes you look at your own selfie and think: what a face. Turn that moment into a picture you can live with. A small act of self-care.

How it works

  1. Upload a sad photoA real one, not posed. The more honest the sadness, the funnier the result.
  2. Tell us a littleWho they are, what makes them laugh, what is off-limits.
  3. The artist paints3–5 days, by hand, on real canvas, taking in everything you told us.
  4. Give itWe ship worldwide from Ukraine with Nova Post. The surprise is yours to give.

Film the moment you give it

The most precious part of this gift isn't the painting. It's the second when someone laughs for the first time in a long while. Catch it on video or in a photo. It's the proof they've been missing: I can still laugh.

  1. Start recording earlyPut your phone on a shelf or ask someone to film. The reaction lasts a few seconds and is easy to miss.
  2. Sad photo first, then the paintingRemind them of that selfie, then unwrap the canvas. The most honest laughs come from the contrast.
  3. Show the video laterA day or a week later, when things get heavy again. Seeing yourself smile works better than any advice.
  4. Don't insistIf they don't want to be filmed, just be there. The moment matters more than the shot.

Want to share the reaction with us? Reply to your confirmation email with the video. We'll only show it on the site if the person in it agrees.

Questions about portraits from photos

Is it really painted by a person, not AI?

Yes, every portrait is painted by an artist, by hand, with real paint on canvas. We don't work on tablets. If you like, the artist will take a selfie with the finished painting before shipping.

What if my friend is offended?

We paint with love: the situation becomes funny (rain, a little cloud, a mountain of dishes), never their looks. The painting comes to you, so you decide when and how to give it. Tell us in the form what is off-limits.

What kind of photo works?

Any photo where you can see the face and the emotion: a selfie on the bus, a shot from under the blanket, a video-call screenshot. Quality doesn't matter, the mood does.

How long does it take?

Usually 3–5 days to paint, or 48 hours with the rush option. International shipping from Ukraine adds about 5–10 days to Europe and the UK and 10–14 days to North America.

What happens to the photo?

Only the artist sees it. We never publish work without permission, and we delete the original once the order is complete.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

We ask you for something very personal: a sad photo and a few words about a person. So here is, in plain words, what happens to it. This policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection".

1. Who we are

The data controller is Sour Mugs (Кислі пички), a small art studio based in Ukraine ("we").

For any question about orders or your data, write to kyslipychki@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • The photo you upload for the portrait.
  • The name of the person in the portrait and the description you write in the form.
  • Your email address, so we can talk about the order.
  • The recipient's country, delivery address and phone number.
  • Payment details: amount and status. We never see or store card numbers, the payment provider handles them.
  • Technical browser data needed for the site to work.
  • Anonymous visit statistics from Google Analytics, only if you allow it in the cookie banner.

3. Why and on what legal basis

  • To paint the portrait, ship it and talk to you about the order (performance of a contract).
  • To take payment and keep accounting records (legal obligation).
  • To understand how the site is used (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

We never send marketing without your separate consent and never sell data to anyone.

4. The photo and the painting

  • Only the artist working on your order sees the photo.
  • The photo is never used to train AI and never shared for that purpose.
  • We delete the original photo within 30 days after the painting is delivered.
  • We may show the finished painting in our gallery or social media only with separate consent from you and the person in the portrait. You can withdraw consent and we will take it down.

5. If the photo shows someone else

By uploading a friend's photo you confirm that you may share it for this purpose, and you decide when and how to give the painting. The person in the portrait can contact us at any time and ask us to delete their photo and data.

6. Sensitive information

Please don't include diagnoses, medication or other health details in "a few words about them": the artist doesn't need them. If such details arrive anyway, we use them only for the portrait and delete them together with the photo.

7. Who we share data with

  • Nova Post and its partner carriers: name, phone and address for delivery.
  • The payment provider: what is needed to process payment.
  • Hosting and email services the site runs on, only as far as they need to work.
  • Google Analytics, if you consent: anonymous visit statistics.
  • Public authorities, where the law requires it.

8. International transfers

We work from Ukraine, and some of our providers (for example Google) process data in the USA. When data leaves the EU or the UK, we rely on adequacy decisions or the standard contractual clauses offered by these providers.

9. How long we keep data

  • The photo and description: up to 30 days after delivery.
  • Contact and order details: as long as tax law requires, then deleted.

10. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, take it elsewhere and withdraw consent. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the ICO in the UK or the authority in your EU country.

11. Cookies

We use only essential cookies and browser storage (for example to remember your cookie choice and currency). Google Analytics cookies are set only after you click "Allow" in the banner. You can change your mind by clearing the site data in your browser.

12. Children

Orders must be placed by adults. Only a parent or legal guardian may send a photo of a child.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top and let customers with active orders know about significant changes.
