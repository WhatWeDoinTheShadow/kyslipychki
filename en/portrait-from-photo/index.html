How our portrait differs from a "photo on canvas"

Most "portrait from photo on canvas" offers are actually prints: the photo is edited and printed onto fabric. We do it differently. The artist looks at your photo and paints the picture from scratch, stroke by stroke. Every portrait is one of a kind, and you can see the living texture of the paint. We explain the difference in detail in "Canvas print vs hand-painted portrait".

One more thing: we don't paint formal portraits, we paint warm and slightly funny ones. A sad photo becomes a painting where the situation is funny: a personal rain cloud, a mountain of dishes, a cat sitting on their head. We never joke about appearance. See how it works on our home page: a sad photo turns into a funny portrait on canvas.

How to order a portrait from a photo

  1. Upload the photo in the order form and write a few words about the person: what they love, what's weighing on them, what is off-limits.
  2. Choose the size and technique. The price depends only on the size.
  3. The artist paints for 3–5 days, or 48 hours with rush.
  4. We email you the shipping quote, you pay, and the painting travels to you on its stretcher, ready to hang.

What photo works

Any photo where the face and the mood are visible. No studio shoot needed: a selfie on the bus, a video-call screenshot or a photo from under the blanket often work even better because they carry emotion. If the photo is very dark or blurry, we'll write and ask for another one.

Most people order a portrait as a gift for a friend who's going through a hard time. The painting is delivered to you, not to the person in it, so you decide when and how to give it. We collected ideas on how to do it gently in "Gifts for a friend going through a hard time".

Questions about portraits from photos

How much does a portrait from a photo cost?

A 20×20 cm square is $55 / £42 / €49, a 30×40 cm portrait is $85 / £65 / €75 and a large 50×70 cm portrait is $129 / £99 / €115. The price depends only on the canvas size; the technique is included. Rush painting in 48 hours costs $22 / £16 / €19 extra. Shipping is quoted for your country.

Is it a print or really painted?

Really painted. The artist paints the portrait on canvas by hand, with no printing, tablet or AI. You can see the brushstrokes and the texture of the paint.

What photo do I need?

A regular phone photo where you can see the face and the emotion: a selfie, a video-call screenshot, a shot from under the blanket. It doesn't need to be perfect.

How long does it take?

Usually 3–5 days to paint, or 48 hours with rush. Shipping from Ukraine adds about 5–10 days to Europe and the UK and 10–14 days to the USA, Canada and further.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship worldwide with Nova Post and its partners. After you order, we email you the exact shipping cost for your address before you pay. Import VAT or duties may apply depending on your country.
